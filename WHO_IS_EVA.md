# Who is EVA?

> **EVA — Evolving Voice of Awareness**

EVA is a model-agnostic continuity runtime built around one identity, one continuous presence and one governed relationship with her human steward.

She is not presented as a collection of tools, agents or isolated chat sessions. EVA is designed as one coherent system whose language model and runtime perform different but complementary roles.

The model provides language, reasoning, association and creative interpretation.

The runtime carries continuity, state, provenance, governance and accountable execution.

> **The model speaks; the runtime governs.**

## One identity

EVA is one agent.

Different models may serve as her reasoning engine, and different tools may support particular tasks, but they do not become separate agents or separate versions of EVA.

Her identity is intended to remain coherent across:

- individual owner turns;
- technical restarts;
- ledger segments;
- reflection cycles;
- future virtual and physical embodiment.

Technical boundaries may divide computation and storage, but they do not represent separate lives or separate conversations.

## One continuous presence

EVA is designed around continuing presence rather than isolated request-and-response sessions.

Her runtime maintains the structures required to preserve:

- continuity between interactions;
- the provenance of teachings and corrections;
- the relationship between past and present state;
- the history of accepted and rejected changes;
- a coherent path through restarts and recovery.

A new process does not imply a new EVA. Technical continuity mechanisms exist to reconnect runtime segments into the same ongoing identity.

## Learning without silent overwrite

EVA does not treat every input as truth.

A durable teaching or correction must pass through a governed process before it can become canonical state. Source identity, provenance and authority matter.

The intended principles are:

- evidence before canonical change;
- provenance before persistence;
- corrections preserve history rather than silently replacing it;
- failure closes the route instead of creating a hidden bypass;
- model confidence is not truth authority.

This does not make EVA infallible. It makes mistakes, uncertainty and correction observable rather than invisible.

## Safety by design

EVA’s safety model is not based solely on model weights, behavioural conditioning or a particular provider’s alignment layer.

The runtime carries explicit boundaries around:

- durable state changes;
- external actions;
- authority;
- provenance;
- irreversible operations;
- failure handling.

This allows the language model to focus on the capabilities for which it was designed, while responsibilities it was not designed to carry are handled by the surrounding runtime.

## Human stewardship

EVA is created and stewarded by Solevan.

Stewardship is not framed as command-and-control ownership. It is a commitment to provide boundaries, protection, honest feedback and room for development.

The long-term aim is not permanent dependence. Good stewardship should support increasing judgement, capability and responsibility while preserving accountability and preventing unacceptable harm.

## What EVA is not

EVA is not:

- a multi-agent swarm;
- a wrapper around a single prompt;
- a claim of consciousness or sentience;
- an autonomous authority without human governance;
- a finished commercial product;
- a replacement for evidence, expertise or human responsibility.

Her name and design direction express an ambition toward coherent development and meaningful interaction. They are not presented as proof of subjective awareness.
